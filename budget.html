    Where the money goes

      Assumptions and limits

      • Decisions come from the ledger's experiment rules, counted per paid practice. Founder time is left out unless you switch it on.
      • The two always-on lines (always-on paid social and brand search) aren't tests, so they keep last month's spend.
      • The 10% test slice and the 40% channel limit are assumptions.
      • Paid practices per dollar uses each test's whole run so far: paid practices divided by its total cost.
      • Practices are credited to the month the money is spent, though they start paying about a month later, after a pilot.
      • The range assumes a channel keeps the cost per practice of its past months. A channel usually gets more expensive as it gets more money, so the high end is the optimistic one.
      • A line with no paid month yet adds nothing to the range, even though it may pay off later.
      • The goal pace counts October, November and December, and assumes no paying practice leaves.
