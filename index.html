    Assumptions

    • Every office, group, date and dollar amount is made up: the lead rows, and the spend for April to September 2026.
    • A paid practice is one office location that started paying by September 30. A group that pays for only some of its offices counts only those; the rest are "expansion pending".
    • A signed account is one customer, whether it's one office or a group of 25.
    • Cost is media or vendor spend plus field costs (booth, travel, food). Founder hours count only when "Include founder time" is on, at the rate you set.
    • Click (UTM) credits the ad or link the booking came from. Self-reported credits the answer to "How did you hear about us?". Blend takes the answer when it names a channel, otherwise the click. Anything else is Unknown and credited to no channel.
    • Each test's leads are tagged to that test when they arrive (a partner code, a booth scan, a postcard code or an ad campaign), so test decisions don't change with the credit setting. The channel table does.
    • If the same office books twice, it counts once, at its earliest touch, and keeps the furthest step either booking reached.
    • Under 3 paid practices (or accounts, when counting accounts) is "too few to call". One lucky deal isn't a channel.
    • The scale and stop bars, minimum wins, guardrails, caps and windows for each test are assumptions, as is the $100 an hour founder rate.
    • Recent spend has had less time to turn into paying practices (a pilot runs about four weeks), so the newest months look more expensive than they'll end up.
    • Money is kept in whole cents and shown in whole dollars, rounded down.

    Questions I'd ask first

    • When a group signs, do you count one customer or one per office, and does price follow offices or providers?
    • When the demo booking's source and the "How did you hear about us?" answer disagree, which do you trust?
    • Do founder hours at lunch-and-learns and conferences count as acquisition cost?
    • What payback period would make you put more money into a channel?
